# Two real learners

Both samples come from **public, anonymised learner corpora whose licences allow them to be shared** — which is why we can show them here and paste them into AI tools. Do **not** do this with your own students' work unless they have agreed.

Read the task each learner was given before you read what they produced. An error is only an error relative to what the learner was trying to do.

## What these samples can and cannot tell you
kind: card
- **Two samples, one moment each.** You see what each learner did on one task, on one day — not everything she can do.
- **Speech is not writing.** Fillers, repetitions and self-corrections are normal in speaking. They are not errors to "correct" — though they can tell you where she was working hard.
- **This is a transcript, not a recording.** You cannot judge pronunciation or speed from it.
- **Start with what each learner does well** before listing what goes wrong.
