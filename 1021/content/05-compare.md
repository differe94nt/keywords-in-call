## Compare the error analyses
kind: questions
- Which errors did **everyone** find? Which did **only one** of you find?
- Did either AI call something an error that is actually acceptable English here?
- **Writing:** did the AIs notice that the assignment asked for one paragraph per solution and a quotation or paraphrase from an outside source?
- **Speaking:** did the AIs treat fillers, repetitions or self-corrections as errors, or make claims about pronunciation they could not hear?
- Did anyone explain an error by the learner's first language? What evidence was there?
- Whose categories were most useful for teaching — not just the most detailed?

## Compare the priorities and teaching plans
kind: questions
- Did the two AIs choose the same priorities as you? If not, whose reason is better?
- Is each teaching plan written for *this* learner, or would it fit any learner?
- Is the feedback type chosen on purpose, with a reason?
- Does each plan check learning with **new** content?
- Did the AI cite a theory or study? **Check that the source exists** — as you did in Week 2.

## Test the HTML page
kind: questions
- Does it work? Try a right answer, a wrong answer and no answer.
- Are all the answer keys correct?
- Does it practise the priority the plan chose — or something easier to build?
- Could this learner use it on her own? What would you change before giving it to a student?

## The corpus annotators' tags · speaking
kind: reveal

| Line | What she said | Annotators' correction | Error type |
|---|---|---|---|
| B3 | It's sunny day | It's **a** sunny day | article |
| B3 | people in this town were very relaxed | … **are** very relaxed | verb tense |
| B3 | in front of house | in front of **a** house | article |
| B3 | The boys are playing ball | … playing **with a** ball | preposition, article |
| B3 | two of housewives | **two housewives** | noun phrase |
| B3 | they feel comfortable very much | they feel **very comfortable** | adverb choice and position |
| B10 | Shimokitazawa is very small town | … is **a** very small town | article |
| B14 | I like to live in city | … in **a** city | article |
| B16 | more than live in countryside | more than **to live** in **a** countryside | verb form, article |
| B18 | city … stimulate me | **a** city **stimulates** me | article, subject–verb agreement |
| B22 | there are many information | there **is much** information | agreement, quantifier |
| B24 | (many information) to get me | … **for me to get** | word order, preposition |
| B26 | I'd like to live countryside | … live **in a** countryside | preposition, article |

**Discuss before you accept it:** eight of the twenty tags are articles. The annotators wrote *a countryside* where most speakers would say *the countryside*, and left *on a roof of a car* and *on sunny Sunday* untagged. Expert annotation is careful — and it is still one reading.

## Points worth checking · writing
kind: reveal
There is no official annotation for Sample A. These are points a teacher would check — a starting point for discussion, not an answer key.
- **Task fulfilment:** all three solutions sit in one paragraph (A3), although the assignment asked for one paragraph per solution. "As the news TV reported…" (A2) gestures at a source without naming one, so the outside-source requirement is not really met.
- **Sentence boundaries and capitals:** sentences often begin without a capital letter after a full stop (A2, A3), and there is often no space after punctuation.
- **Spelling:** *improtant, easilly, perparaed, alseep, benifit, litter* (for *little*), *fell* (for *feel*).
- **Verb forms and patterns:** *it will effects us*; *advise us read*; *listen some light music*; *help you for sleep*; *make you more relax*.
- **Missing subjects and fragments:** *Then will easily alseep*; *when you read so time*.
- **Number and quantity:** *90percent person*; *some solution*; *some opinion*.
- **Register:** the closing emoticon *^.^* is informal for an essay.
- **Strengths:** a clear problem–solution structure with sequencing words (*firstly, secondly, lastly*), three sensible solutions, and a reason given for each.
