# Validity & measurement

Every quiz, rubric and AI score makes a claim about learners. Validity asks **whether the evidence supports that claim**; reliability asks **how much of the score is error**. These papers are the measurement backbone of everything else this week — and of **W13, Digital assessment and test design**.
