## Borrow the method
kind: card
A **scientometric review** (document co-citation analysis — Aryadoust (2020) used the CiteSpace package) is a realistic method for a final-project literature review. Lim & Aryadoust (2022) and Aryadoust (2020) show each step: search, cluster, label, then read the most influential papers in each cluster.
