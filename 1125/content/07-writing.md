## Try it
kind: card
Run one paragraph through an automated feedback tool and through a generative AI. Sort every comment into **form** or **meaning**. Which tool helps with content and organisation — and how would you know its feedback is accurate?

## Discuss
kind: questions
- When would you use an automated score in your teaching, and when would you refuse to?
