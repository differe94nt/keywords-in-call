# Sources

Fifteen papers by Vahid Aryadoust and colleagues. Every reference was checked against its DOI record (Crossref) on 23 November 2026, and each summary card says whether it was written from the full text or from the abstract only. Free full texts are linked only where they are legal: the publisher's open-access version or the author's accepted manuscript in the NIE repository.

- **Book chapters.** Both chapters were published in 2026. The publisher's page for *Effective Assessment in Schools* shows ©2027, but the book appeared in July 2026, so we cite 2026, the date in the DOI record.

- **Background facts** about the author come from his NTU library profile, the NIE department page, ORCID and Google Scholar (checked 23 November 2026).
